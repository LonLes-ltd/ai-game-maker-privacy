Privacy Policy

Ai Game Maker · Effective date: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LonLes Ltd (“LonLes,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Ai Game Maker mobile application and related game-creation features (the “App”).

At a glance

The App does not require you to create an account and does not offer in-app purchases. GitHub integration is optional. Game prompts and project content are processed to provide game creation, previews, and any publishing action you request.

1. Information we process

  • Prompts and project content. When you ask the App to create or update a game, we process the text you submit and related project files or settings needed to fulfill that request. Do not include passwords, private keys, or other secrets in prompts or project content.
  • Games and publishing choices. We process game output and project details needed to show previews, save or update your work, and publish or share it when you choose to do so. Content you publish may be visible to other people through the relevant public game or sharing feature.
  • Technical and usage information. Our App and service infrastructure may receive connection and request information, such as IP address, device or browser characteristics, app/service events, and diagnostic records, to deliver the service, protect it, and troubleshoot failures.
  • Optional GitHub integration. If you choose to connect GitHub, the App uses information made available through GitHub’s authorization flow and the permissions you approve to perform the GitHub actions you request. GitHub separately processes information under its own privacy policy. You can disconnect or revoke the App’s access in GitHub settings.

2. How we use information

We use information to generate and update games, provide previews and sharing or publishing features, operate and secure the App, diagnose technical issues, prevent abuse, and respond to support or legal requests. We do not require account registration to use the App, and the App does not offer in-app purchases.

3. Service providers and sharing

To provide game generation and hosting, the prompt and project content relevant to your request may be sent to the AI, hosting, storage, or other technical service providers used to operate that feature. Those providers process information on our behalf or under their own applicable terms. If you connect GitHub, information is exchanged with GitHub to perform the actions you authorize. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information where required by law, to protect users or the service, or in connection with a business transfer.

4. Public content

Only publish content you are comfortable sharing. A game or project you choose to publish or share may be accessible to other users or anyone with its link. Do not put personal information or confidential material in public prompts, source files, or game content.

5. Retention and deletion requests

We retain prompts and project content for as long as needed to provide the features you use, maintain your saved or published projects, and protect the service. Technical records may be kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting. To ask us to delete information or a project associated with your use of the App, email contact@omgithub.com and describe the information or project. We may retain information where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, dispute resolution, or backup recovery. Public copies or links shared by others may not be under our control.

6. Security and transfers

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to applicable safeguards and laws.

7. Children

The App is not designed to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us at contact@omgithub.com so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

8. Your choices

You can use the App without creating an account. You can choose not to connect GitHub, and you can revoke GitHub access through GitHub’s settings. You can also contact us to ask about access to or deletion of information, subject to applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or our practices change. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the effective date. Review this page periodically.
